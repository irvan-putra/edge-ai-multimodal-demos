Edge AI Demo Lab

Browser editions of the live demos from Edge AI & Multimodal Applications in Python - PythonID Jakarta, 31 Oct 2026. Everything below runs on your device; models download once, then inference is local.

Demo 0 - voice
Hands-free slide control

Whisper running in your browser (transformers.js). Say "Lanjut" or "Kembali" - the mini deck obeys.

 Demo 1 - vision
The camera counts the room

MediaPipe pose on your webcam: counts people, detects raised hands. Feeds Demo 2 live.

 Demo 2 - dashboard
Raised hands become a dashboard

Real Streamlit, running in Pyodide in your browser (stlite). Reads Demo 1's counts, simulates when idle.

 Demo 3 - sensors
Phone motion over MQTT

Your phone's accelerometer published over MQTT (public broker) to a live event feed with shake detection.
First load needs internet (models from public CDNs). After that, Demos 0-2 run fully on-device. Demo 3 relays through a free public broker - no data is stored anywhere. Python originals: see the repo README.
